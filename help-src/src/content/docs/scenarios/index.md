:::tip[Save this for last]
Scenarios are one of the last features to explore. They're only as good as the picture they start from, so first add all your accounts, debts, and [balances](/help/accounts/recording-balances/) to build a complete net worth. Then come back to plan.
:::

A scenario is a "what if" plan built on your real accounts. It projects your net worth from today through **life expectancy**, spending down your savings once you retire, so you can see whether your money lasts. Try retiring earlier, taking a sabbatical, buying a home, or receiving an inheritance, and compare the results.

Scenarios never change your real accounts. They're assumptions layered on top of them, so your balances stay up to date as you record them.

:::note
Scenarios are part of [WealthTrunk Pro](/help/subscription/wealthtrunk-pro/).
:::

## Where to find them

- **iPhone:** the **Planner** tab has **Goals** and **Scenarios** pages.
- **iPad and Mac:** **Scenarios** is its own tab, with your list beside the selected scenario.

Scenarios build on your accounts, so add your savings, investments, and debts first. A person needs at least one asset included in projections, and one liquid asset like cash or investments, before you can plan for them.

## Creating a scenario

1. **Scenarios** → **+** (or **Create a Plan** the first time).
2. Choose whose plan it is: **Joint** (you and your partner) or one person. Dependents can have a scenario too, for their savings years.
3. Tap **Create**, or tap an example name like **Simple Retirement** or **Work Longer** to start with that name. An example only sets the name.

The new scenario opens right away. Name, ages, spending, and everything else are changed from there.

A new scenario starts with:

- Your current **Retirement Age** and a **Life Expectancy** of 85 (or a year past retirement, if later). Dependents plan to 25.
- A **Living Expenses** spending that begins at retirement, if you've set **Monthly Spending** in your [FIRE settings](/help/projections/net-worth-projections/#fire).
- The app's inflation rate, or 3% if **Show in Today's Money** is off.
- The **Linked** projection type. See [Linked vs. Manual](/help/scenarios/linked-vs-manual/).

## The scenario screen

From top to bottom:

1. **Alerts**, only when something needs attention. See [Reading your results](/help/scenarios/reading-results/).
2. The **chart**. Drag across it to see any year, or tap it for the year-by-year list.
3. One card for your inputs. Tap the chips in its header to switch between **Income**, **Spending**, **Assets**, and **Liabilities**, and use **+** to add to the one you're on.

Along the top:

- The **umbrella** button opens the **Timeline**: retirement ages and life expectancy. See [Timeline and assumptions](/help/scenarios/timeline-and-assumptions/).
- **Edit** opens the name, **Projection Type**, inflation, returns, tax, and note.

## Primary scenario

One scenario per person (or couple) can be **Primary**, marked with a gold umbrella. Your first scenario for someone is primary automatically.

A primary scenario:

- Extends the Dashboard's [**Balance Projection**](/help/projections/net-worth-projections/) through retirement and spending, when its people are the ones selected
- Sets each person's **Retirement Age** in their profile. Changing the age in the scenario changes the profile.
- Drives the projection chart in your [PDF statement](/help/dashboard/pdf-statements/)

Use **Make Primary** or **Remove Primary** from the scenario's menu (long press or **•••**) or from the Timeline. Other scenarios keep their own ages, so you can compare freely without touching your profile.

## Managing scenarios

- **Duplicate** a scenario before changing it, to compare the two. This is the quickest way to answer "what if we retire three years earlier?"
- **Archive** ones you're done with. Show them again from **Show Archived** in the **•••** menu.
- **Sort By** last retirement year, ending value, spending, or date created, and **Group By** owner.
- Add a **Note** in **Edit** to remember what a scenario was testing.

Edits save as you make them. Scenarios are included in [backups](/help/data/backup-restore/) and sync through iCloud.
