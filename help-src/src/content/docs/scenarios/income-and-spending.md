Income and spending are the building blocks of a scenario. In Manual they drive saving. In Linked they cover what your accounts don't: retirement spending, pensions, and one-time events.

## Adding one

1. On the scenario, tap the **Income** or **Spending** chip.
2. Tap **+** and choose a starting point, or **Custom**.
3. Set the **Amount**, **Frequency**, and when it **Starts** and **Ends**.

Changes save as you go.

### Starting points

| Income | Spending |
| --- | --- |
| **Salary** (until retirement) | **Living Expenses** (from retirement) |
| **Government Pension**, like Social Security (from retirement) | **Housing** |
| **Workplace Pension** (from retirement) | **Health Care** (from retirement) |
| **Part-Time Work** (from retirement) | **Travel** (from retirement) |
| **Rental Income** | **Education** |
| **Inheritance** (once) | **Charity** |
| **Custom** | **Vehicle Purchase** (once) |
| | **Large Purchase** (once) |
| | **Custom** |

A starting point only fills in a name, symbol, and timing. Every value can be changed, and nothing assumes your country's rules.

Tap the symbol beside the name to choose another from the searchable picker.

## Frequency

- **Monthly:** every month, like rent or a pension payment
- **Yearly:** once a year, like a bonus, a tuition bill, or an annual trip
- **Once:** one time, like an inheritance or a car. It has a start and no end.

## Starts and Ends

Timing follows milestones rather than fixed dates, so it moves when you change a retirement age.

| Choice | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Now** | The start of the plan |
| **Name Retires** | The day after that person's retirement year ends |
| **First Retirement** and **Last Retirement** | Joint scenarios: the earlier and later retirement. Spending usually starts at the last. |
| **End of Plan** | Ends only. Runs to life expectancy. |
| **Debt Paid Off** | **Linked only.** When a loan reaches zero, which follows your payment. |
| **At an Age…** | A person reaches an age |
| **On a Date…** | A month and year |

For example, a **Salary** that ends at **Retires** stops by itself if you move the retirement age. A **Health Care** event that **Starts** at **Retires** and **Ends** at **At an Age… 65** covers the gap before other coverage begins.

A one-time event dated in the past doesn't happen.

## Annual change

Recurring amounts change each year. With inflation on, the slider is **± vs. Inflation**, so 0% keeps pace with prices. With inflation off it's **Annual Change**.

- A **Salary** that rises 1% faster than prices: **+1%**
- A pension with no cost-of-living adjustments: a negative value, so it loses buying power
- A **fixed loan payment**: set it to the negative of the inflation rate, which the footer calls out, so the payment stays level

## Pause without deleting

Tap an income or spending's symbol in the list, or the **eye** in its editor, to leave it out. This is handy for comparing: pause a pension and see what the plan looks like without it.

## Organize

- **Duplicate** from the editor's toolbar. Useful for a second, similar amount, like another year of tuition.
- **Reorder** (the button beside **+**) lets you drag items into your own order. Otherwise they're listed by when they start.
- **Delete** from the editor. This can't be undone.

## Living Expenses

If you set **Monthly Spending** in your [FIRE settings](/help/projections/net-worth-projections/#fire), a new scenario starts with a **Living Expenses** spending from retirement. Edit it to match what you expect to spend. In a scenario for two people it starts at the last retirement, when both have stopped working.
