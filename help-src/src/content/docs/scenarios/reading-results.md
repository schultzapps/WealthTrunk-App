## The chart

- **Drag** across the chart to see your balance in any year, with the ages and milestones at that point.
- **Tap** the chart to open the **year-by-year list**.
- Tap the chart's **title** to switch between **Net Worth**, **Assets**, **Liabilities**, and **Liquid Assets**. Manual scenarios only chart net worth.
- The range chips under the chart switch between your **saving years**, your **drawdown years**, and **ALL**.
- In the year-by-year list, the toolbar button switches between a **line** and **bars**. Net worth bars show assets going up and liabilities going down.

Markers on the chart show what happens when: retirements, debts paid off, purchases and sales, and the year liquid savings run out. If only illiquid assets like a home remain, the marker shows how much.

## Year-by-year list

Tap any year, then use **Previous Year** and **Next Year** to step through. Year Details has:

| Section | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Change in Net Worth** | How much it rose or fell that year |
| **Balances** | Year-end balances of each account |
| **Cash Flow** | **Income**, **Spending**, **Contributions** or **Saved from Income**, **Growth**, **Withdrawals**, **Withdrawal Rate**, and **Taxes** |
| **Buying & Selling** | What was bought, sold, borrowed, and paid off, plus **Closing & Selling Costs** and **Payments** on loans a scenario adds |
| **Income & Spending** | Each item's amount that year |
| **Milestones** | What happens in the scenario that year |

Linked scenarios show **Contributions** and Manual scenarios show **Saved from Income**.

**Withdrawal Rate** is what you took from liquid assets as a share of them at the start of the year. Rates around 4% are often cited as sustainable over long retirements, but this is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee.

## Alerts

The **Alerts** card lists what needs attention, most serious first. Each is a prompt to check an assumption, not a verdict.

| Alert | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Liquid assets run out in [year]** | Spending outpaces savings. Try later retirement, less spending, or a higher return. |
| **Net worth runs out in [year]** | Manual: the same, for your whole net worth |
| **...run out before drawdown begins** | Savings run out before the last person retires. Check that spending now isn't higher than income. |
| **Not enough cash for [account] in [year]** | A purchase or payoff can't be covered by liquid assets on its date. Lower the price or down payment, or move the date later. |
| **Add spending to see how long your savings last** | The scenario has no spending, so the money never runs out |
| **No income is added, so nothing is saved before retirement** | Manual: add a salary |
| **The payment on [account] doesn't cover its interest** | A debt would never be paid off. Raise its payment. |
| **Retirement age for [name] is at or past life expectancy** | Fix the Timeline |
| **Your current net worth is negative** | Debts exceed assets |

:::note
A projection is an estimate from steady returns and your assumptions. Real markets vary, so look at how a plan responds to a lower return or higher spending, not only the base case. WealthTrunk doesn't give financial advice.
:::
