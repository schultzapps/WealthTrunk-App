## Adding a goal

1. **Goals** tab → **+**.
2. Choose **Net Worth**, **Savings**, or **Debt**, or start from an example like Emergency Fund or Car Loan.
3. Enter a **Goal Name** and **Target Amount**.
4. Choose **Linked** or **Manual**, and pick accounts for a linked goal.
5. Optionally set a **Target Date**, then **Save**.

## Linked vs. Manual

- **Linked:** progress updates from your account balances. Savings goals link to assets; Debt goals to liabilities.
- **Manual:** you update the balance yourself.

## Partial accounts

For Savings goals, tap a linked account to count only a percentage of it. Useful when one account funds several goals.

## Target dates

With a **Target Date**, the goal shows a **Projected Date** and roughly how much more per month you'd need.

## More
