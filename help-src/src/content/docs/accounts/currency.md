## Default currency

**Settings → Currency**. Totals, charts, goals, and projections all show in this currency.

**System Default** is on to start, using your device's region. Turn it off to pick any currency.

## Accounts in other currencies

Each account can have its own currency, like a euro savings account or a pension in pounds.

1. When adding or editing an account, tap **Currency**.
2. Choose from **Recently Used**, **Popular**, or search **All**.

The account keeps its balance in its own currency. Everywhere it's added up with your other accounts, it's converted to your default currency.

On the account's page, tap the balance to flip between its own currency and your default currency.

## Exchange rates

Rates update once a day from [ExchangeRate-API](https://www.exchangerate-api.com) and are saved on your device, so conversions still work offline.

Converted totals, including past balances on charts, use the latest rate. Changing rates can move your net worth even when no balances change.
