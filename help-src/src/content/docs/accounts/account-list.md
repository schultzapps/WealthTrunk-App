The top of the **Accounts** tab shows a balance history chart with chips above it.

## Chips

Tap a chip to choose what the chart and the list show: **Net Worth** for everything, or one of the list's groups, like **Cash** or **Investments**. The chart then charts that group on its own and the list below narrows to match.

Drag across the chart to see a balance on a date. Change the date range with the picker above it.

## Hide the chips and chart

If you'd rather see a plain list, turn off **Settings → Charts & Lists → Show Overview**. Turn it on to bring them back. The setting syncs across your devices.
