## Adding an account

1. **Accounts** tab → **+** (Mac: **⌘ N**).
2. Search for your bank under **Find or add an institution**. For property, enter a name instead, like "Our Home".
3. Choose the [**Account Type**](/help/accounts/account-types/) and, if needed, the [**Tax Status**](/help/accounts/tax-types/).
4. Choose the owner. Pick two for a joint account.
5. Enter the balance and **Balance Date**, then **Save**.

## Projection details

- **Assets:** **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**
- **Liabilities:** **Loan Rate (APR)** and **Monthly Payment**
- **Include In Projections:** turn off to leave the account out

You can also set a **Nickname**, **Currency**, **Notes**, and a logo or emoji.

## Many accounts?

[Import them from CSV](/help/accounts/import-csv/).

## Closing an account

Open it → **Edit** → **Close** → **Close Account**. The balance becomes 0 and it's left out of projections, but its history stays. To reopen it, choose **Show Closed Accounts** from the **•••** menu, open it, and tap **Edit** → **Reopen**.
