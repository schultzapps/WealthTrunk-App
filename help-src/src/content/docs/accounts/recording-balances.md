Once your accounts are set up, recording balances is the main thing you'll do in WealthTrunk. Every update adds a dated balance to each account's history, and together they build your net worth history.

## How often to update

Whatever you'll stick with. Most people update once a month, often after payday or when statements arrive. Weekly works if you like watching closely; quarterly is fine if you'd rather check in less often. Consistency matters more than frequency.

Set up [reminders](#reminders) so you don't have to remember.

## Your progress over time

One update is a snapshot. A year of updates is a story. Over time, your [Balance History](/help/dashboard/customize-dashboard/) and **Monthly Net Worth** charts show how your wealth is growing, how fast debts are shrinking, and what changed month to month.

Update every account on the same day when you can. Your charts will be more accurate.

## Update all accounts

1. **Accounts** tab → **Update All** (Mac: **⌘ B**).
2. Enter each new balance. If one hasn't changed, tap its previous balance above the keyboard.
3. Tap **Done**.

## Update one account

Open the account and tap the **pencil** next to **Account Balance**.

## Siri, Spotlight, and Shortcuts

- **Siri:** *"Update my balance in WealthTrunk"*, or name the account: *"Update Chase Checking balance in WealthTrunk"*.
- **Spotlight:** search **Update Balance**.
- **Shortcuts:** use the **Update Account Balance** action.

## Action Button or Back Tap

Record a balance from anywhere with the iPhone **Action button**, or with **Back Tap** by tapping the back of your iPhone.

1. In **Shortcuts**, create a shortcut with WealthTrunk's **Update Account Balance** action. Leave **Account** and **Amount** empty to be asked each time.
2. Assign it:
   - **Action button:** iPhone **Settings → Action Button → Shortcut**, then select your shortcut.
   - **Back Tap:** iPhone **Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap**, choose **Double Tap** or **Triple Tap**, then select your shortcut.

## Reminders

**Settings → Notifications → Net Worth Reminders**. Choose frequency, day, and time.

## Keep balances fresh

An old balance makes your net worth, charts, and projections less accurate. To spot accounts that are behind:

- **Highlight Stale Accounts** (**Settings → Charts & Lists**): colors the "last updated" date of accounts older than 1 week, 1 month, 6 months (default), or 1 year. Set it to match how often you update.
- **Sort By → Last Updated** in the Accounts **•••** menu.
- **Staleness Indicator** (**Settings → Charts & Lists**): a dashed line on Balance History when your latest update is over 7 days old.
