Every account has a **Tax Status** that describes how it's taxed. New assets start as **After-Tax** and new liabilities as **None**. Change it when you add or edit the account, for example to **Tax-Deferred** for a 401(k).

The **Tax Categories** card on the Dashboard uses tax status to show how your wealth splits by tax treatment. This helps you see how much of your money you can spend freely and how much will be taxed when you withdraw it.

## Built-in tax types

| Tax type | How it works | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **After-Tax** | Pay as you go. Taxes apply each year to interest or profits. | Checking, Savings, Brokerage |
| **Tax-Deferred** | Pay later. You get a tax break now, but pay tax when you withdraw. | Traditional IRA, 401(k) |
| **Roth** | Pay now. Contributions are taxed today, but withdrawals are tax-free later. | Roth IRA |
| **Tax-Advantaged** | Goal-specific. Special tax benefits for things like healthcare or education. | HSA, 529 |
| **Mixed** | Combined. Use this when one account holds more than one kind, such as Roth and Tax-Deferred balances. | A 401(k) with Roth and traditional contributions |
| **None** | No tax treatment applies. | Mortgages, loans, credit cards |

## Custom tax types

If your situation doesn't fit, for example a tax-free account in your country, create your own:

1. Choose **Tax Status** while adding or editing an account and create a new type from the picker, or go to **Settings → Custom Accounts → Tax Types**.
2. Enter a name and save.

In **Settings → Custom Accounts**, each custom tax type shows how many accounts use it. You can edit or delete it there, but not while an account is still using it.
