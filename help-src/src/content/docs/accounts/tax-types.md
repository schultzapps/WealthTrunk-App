New assets default to **After-Tax** and liabilities to **None**. The **Tax Categories** card groups your wealth by tax type.

## Built-in tax types

| Tax type | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **After-Tax** | Taxed yearly on interest or gains | Checking, Savings, Brokerage |
| **Tax-Deferred** | Tax break now, taxed on withdrawal | Traditional IRA, 401(k) |
| **Roth** | Taxed now, tax-free withdrawals | Roth IRA |
| **Tax-Advantaged** | Special benefits for health or education | HSA, 529 |
| **Mixed** | More than one type in one account | 401(k) with Roth and traditional |
| **None** | No tax treatment | Mortgages, loans, credit cards |

## Custom tax types

Create one from the **Tax Status** picker or **Settings → Custom Accounts → Tax Types**. A type can't be deleted while an account uses it.
