WealthTrunk can track every part of your finances:

- **Cash:** checking, savings, money market
- **Investments:** brokerage, retirement accounts
- **Property:** homes, vehicles, collectibles. These use their own name instead of a bank name.
- **Future Income:** pensions, annuities, and lifetime payouts. A built-in calculator estimates the lump-sum value of the monthly income. Tap **Apply** to use that estimate as the account's balance.
- **Liabilities:** mortgages, loans, credit cards

If none of these fit, create your own account type or tax type right from the picker.
