## Liquid vs. illiquid

Illiquid types: Real Estate, Vehicle, Collectibles, Private Equity, pensions, Annuity, and Other Asset. All other assets are liquid. The FIRE card counts only liquid assets unless you change it in the card's settings.

## Custom account types

Create one from the **Account Type** picker or **Settings → Custom Accounts → Account Types**. Give it a name, choose **Asset** or **Liability**, and for assets, whether it's **Liquid**. A type can't be deleted while an account uses it.

## Pensions and annuities

Enter the lump-sum value if you have one. Otherwise, tap **f(x)** in the toolbar to open the **Future Income Calculator**, then **Apply**. Future Income counts toward net worth but isn't projected.
