## Assets

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Checking, Savings, High Yield Savings, Money Market, Cash |
| **Investments** | Brokerage, Retirement, HSA, Crypto, Trust Account, ESPP, Private Equity, Precious Metals |
| **Education** | 529 Education, Coverdell ESA |
| **Custodial** | UTMA/UGMA, Custodial Brokerage |
| **Property** | Real Estate, Vehicle, Collectibles |
| **Future Income** | Employer Pension, State Pension, Annuity |
| **Other Assets** | Other Asset, Other Liquid Asset |

## Liabilities

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit Cards** | Credit Card |
| **Mortgages** | Mortgage |
| **Loans** | Vehicle Loan, Personal Loan, Student Loan, HELOC, Business Loan |
| **Other Liabilities** | Tax Liability, Medical Debt, Family Loan, Other Liability |

## Net Worth Breakdown card

The **Net Worth Breakdown** [Dashboard card](/help/dashboard/customize-dashboard/) shows an **Assets** bar and a **Liabilities** bar, each split by the groups above. Switch between **%** and amounts at the top.

Use it to see where your wealth sits at a glance: how much is in cash vs. investments vs. property, and which debts make up the most of what you owe.

## Liquid vs. illiquid

Illiquid types: Real Estate, Vehicle, Collectibles, Private Equity, pensions, Annuity, and Other Asset. All other assets are liquid. The FIRE card counts only liquid assets unless you change it in the card's settings.

## Custom account types

Create one from the **Account Type** picker or **Settings → Custom Accounts → Account Types**. Give it a name, choose **Asset** or **Liability**, and for assets, whether it's **Liquid**. A type can't be deleted while an account uses it.

## Pensions and annuities

Enter the lump-sum value if you have one. Otherwise, tap **f(x)** in the toolbar to open the **Future Income Calculator**, then **Apply**. Future Income counts toward net worth but isn't projected.
