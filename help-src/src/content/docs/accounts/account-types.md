Pick an **Account Type** whenever you add an account. The type decides which group the account appears in and how it's charted and projected.

## Assets

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| **Cash** | Checking, Savings, High Yield Savings, Money Market, Cash |
| **Investments** | Brokerage, Retirement, HSA, Crypto, Trust Account, ESPP, Private Equity |
| **Education** | 529 Education, Coverdell ESA |
| **Custodial** | UTMA/UGMA, Custodial Brokerage |
| **Property** | Real Estate, Vehicle, Collectibles |
| **Future Income** | Employer Pension, State Pension, Annuity |
| **Other Assets** | Other Asset, Other Liquid Asset |

## Liabilities

| Group | Types |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit Cards** | Credit Card |
| **Mortgages** | Mortgage |
| **Loans** | Vehicle Loan, Personal Loan, Student Loan, HELOC, Business Loan |
| **Other Liabilities** | Tax Liability, Medical Debt, Family Loan, Other Liability |

## Tax status

Each account also has a **Tax Status**: After-Tax, Tax-Deferred, Roth, Tax-Advantaged, Mixed, or None. The **Tax Categories** chart on the Dashboard uses it to show how your wealth splits by tax treatment.

## Custom types

If nothing fits, create your own account type or tax type right from the picker, or manage them in **Settings → Custom Accounts**. Custom types are free for everyone.

## Future Income: pensions and annuities

Pensions and annuities pay income in the future rather than holding a balance, so WealthTrunk converts them into a lump-sum (present) value:

- If your provider gave you a lump-sum or cash value, enter it as the balance.
- Otherwise, tap the **f(x)** button in the toolbar while adding or updating the account. The **Future Income Calculator** estimates the value from your monthly income, how long it's paid, and a discount rate. Tap **Apply** to use the result.

Future Income accounts count toward your net worth but are left out of growth projections.

## Closing an account

To stop tracking an account without losing its history, open it, tap **Edit**, tap **Close**, and confirm with **Close Account**. Its balance becomes 0 and it's left out of future projections. To find it again, choose **Show Closed Accounts** from the **•••** menu on the Accounts tab; you can **Reopen** it from its Edit screen.
