You can add **holdings** to any account: stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, money market funds, and crypto. WealthTrunk looks up live market prices for them.

- With one tap, update the account's balance to match the market value of its holdings.
- See your mix of investments on the Dashboard's **Portfolio Allocation** chart (Pro).
- Export your holdings to CSV from **Import/Export Data**.

Holdings are optional. Many people just record the account's total balance.
