**Track Holdings** is an optional Pro feature. On the free plan you can search tickers and preview their value; saving a holding requires Pro. It lets you track the individual investments inside an account, such as stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, money market funds, and crypto, using live prices.

## Turn it on

1. Open the account and tap **Edit**, or turn it on while adding a new account.
2. Turn on **Track Holdings** and tap **Save**.

## Add a holding

1. In the account's holdings list, tap **Add Holding**.
2. Search by ticker or name, for example `AAPL`, `VTSAX`, or `BTC-USD`.
3. Enter the **Quantity** and save.

Prices come from Yahoo Finance and refresh automatically once they're more than a few minutes old.

## Sync to Account

Holdings don't change your account balance automatically. Tap **Sync to Account** to record a balance for today equal to the current market value of the account's holdings.

This is deliberately manual. Your data stays private and on your devices, and you decide when a balance is recorded.

## See your allocation

Add the **Portfolio Allocation** card to your Dashboard to see your holdings across every account, as a tree map.

Holdings are included when you [export to CSV](/help/data/export/).
