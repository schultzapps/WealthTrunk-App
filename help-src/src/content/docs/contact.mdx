Can't find what you're looking for? We're happy to help.

## From the app (best)

Go to **Settings → Contact Us** and choose **Get Help** or **Report a Problem**. This automatically includes your anonymous customer ID, which makes it much easier to help with app-related issues. WealthTrunk doesn't collect your name or email, so we can't contact you first.

## By email

<ContactLink label="Email support" />. If you can, include your device, its iOS or macOS version, and your WealthTrunk version.

## Other ways to get in touch

- **Billing and refunds** are handled by Apple. Contact Apple Support.
- **Feature ideas:** [suggest a feature or vote on others' ideas](https://wealthtrunk.userjot.com/), or use **Settings → Suggest Feature** in the app.
- **What's changed:** read the [release notes](https://wealthtrunk.app/release-notes.html), or **Settings → What's New** in the app.
