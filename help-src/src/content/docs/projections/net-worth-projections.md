The **Balance Projection** card on the Dashboard shows where your whole household is headed. It adds up every [account projection](/help/projections/account-projections/) into one line, month by month.

## How it's calculated

1. Each account included in projections is forecast on its own: assets grow with their **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**, and debts shrink with their **Loan Rate** and **Monthly Payment**.
2. Every month, those forecasts are converted to your [default currency](/help/accounts/currency/) and added together. Debts are subtracted.
3. A paid-off debt stays at zero, and an asset stops getting contributions at its owner's [Retirement Age](/help/family/family-profiles/#retirement-age-and-savings-age) but keeps growing.

Only accounts with **Include In Projections** on are counted, so excluded accounts like checking, closed accounts, and pensions are left out.

## How far it goes

The projection runs to the end of the year the last primary profile reaches their **Retirement Age**.

For example, if you're 40 with a Retirement Age of 65, and your spouse is 35 with a Retirement Age of 62, you retire in 25 years and your spouse in 27. The projection runs 27 years, to the end of the year your spouse turns 62.

## What the card shows

- **Title:** what's projected, like **Projected Net Worth**. The big number is the value at the end of the projection.
- **Net Worth, Assets, Liabilities, or Liquid Assets:** tap a card in **Net Worth Cards** at the top of the Dashboard to switch the projection to match. If that card is hidden, tap the projection's title to choose.
- **Scrub:** drag across the chart to see the projected value for any month.
- **Stats:** the number at the top right shows ages, contributions, and more. See [Projection stats](/help/projections/projection-stats/).

The card follows your [profile selection](/help/getting-started/finding-your-way/#profile-selection). Select just you to see your own projection, or you and your spouse for the household.

## Today's money

**Settings → Charts & Lists → Inflation Settings** → set **Inflation Rate** and turn on **Show in Today's Money**. Example: $1M in 30 years at 2.5% inflation shows as about $477k.

:::caution
Don't subtract inflation from **Expected Return**. It would be counted twice.
:::

## Retirement income

The projection stops at retirement and doesn't subtract withdrawals. To estimate the income your projected balance could support, use the [Withdrawal stat](/help/projections/projection-stats/#withdrawal) or the FIRE card.

## FIRE

- **FIRE number** = monthly spending × 12 ÷ withdrawal rate
- **Coast FIRE** = what you need invested today to reach your FIRE number by retirement with no more contributions
- Tap the card's title to switch; tap the **gear** to set **Monthly Spending** and withdrawal rate.

FIRE is a simplified estimate (one blended return, no taxes), so it won't exactly match other projections.

## Already retired?

Projections need a retirement age in the future. Hide the projection cards if they don't apply.
