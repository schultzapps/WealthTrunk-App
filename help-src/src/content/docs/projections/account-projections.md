Each account is projected month by month from its balance, rate, and contributions, out to the latest **Retirement Age** among primary profiles. All your projected accounts then add up to your [net worth projection](/help/projections/net-worth-projections/).

## Inputs

- **Assets:** **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**
- **Liabilities:** **Loan Rate (APR)** and **Monthly Payment**

## Choosing which accounts to project

**Include In Projections** is on for new accounts. To change it, open the account → **Edit** → **Include In Projections**.

Turn it off for money you spend rather than grow. A checking account pays your day-to-day bills, so it isn't really part of your long-term wealth the way a retirement or savings account is. Projecting it would assume that balance keeps growing for decades.

Excluded accounts still count toward today's net worth. They're just left out of projected balances.

## Growth and Payoff Calculator

Open an account, tap its projection chart, then tap **Calculator** (top right). Assets open the **Growth Calculator** and liabilities the **Payoff Calculator**.

Try a different return, contribution, payment, or retirement age and compare it to your current projection. Tap **Close** to discard, or **Save** to apply the changes. A new retirement age applies to all accounts.

## Growing contributions

The slider under **Monthly Contribution** sets a yearly change: **Annual Change** with inflation off, **± vs. Inflation** with it on. Leave it at 0% unless you expect contributions to grow.

## Partners and dependents

- **Joint accounts:** contributions continue until the last primary profile retires.
- **Individual accounts:** contributions stop at the owner's retirement, but growth continues.
- **Dependents** use their own **Savings Age**.

To see projections in today's dollars, see [Today's money](/help/projections/net-worth-projections/#todays-money).
