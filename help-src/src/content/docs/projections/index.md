---
title: Account projections
description: How WealthTrunk projects each account and your net worth, and how to see it in today's money.
---
Each account is projected from its balance, rate, and contributions to the latest **Retirement Age** among primary profiles.

For example, if you're 40 with a Retirement Age of 65, and your spouse is 35 with a Retirement Age of 62, you retire in 25 years and your spouse in 27. Projections run 27 years, to the end of the year your spouse turns 62.

## Inputs

- **Assets:** **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**
- **Liabilities:** **Loan Rate (APR)** and **Monthly Payment**
- **Include In Projections:** off to exclude an account

## Growth and Payoff Calculator

Open an account, tap its projection chart, then tap **Calculator** (top right). Assets open the **Growth Calculator** and liabilities the **Payoff Calculator**.

Try a different return, contribution, payment, or retirement age and compare it to your current projection. Tap **Close** to discard, or **Save** to apply the changes. A new retirement age applies to all accounts.

## Growing contributions

The slider under **Monthly Contribution** sets a yearly change: **Annual Change** with inflation off, **± vs. Inflation** with it on. Leave it at 0% unless you expect contributions to grow.

## Today's money

**Settings → Charts & Lists → Inflation Settings** → set **Inflation Rate** and turn on **Show in Today's Money**. Example: $1M in 30 years at 2.5% inflation shows as about $477k.

:::caution
Don't subtract inflation from **Expected Return**. It would be counted twice.
:::

## Partners and dependents

- **Joint accounts:** contributions continue until the last primary profile retires.
- **Individual accounts:** contributions stop at the owner's retirement, but growth continues.
- **Dependents** use their own **Savings Age**.

## FIRE

- **FIRE number** = monthly spending × 12 ÷ withdrawal rate
- **Coast FIRE** = what you need invested today to reach your FIRE number by retirement with no more contributions
- Tap the card's title to switch; tap the **gear** to set **Monthly Spending** and withdrawal rate.

FIRE is a simplified estimate (one blended return, no taxes), so it won't exactly match other projections.

## Already retired?

Projections need a retirement age in the future. Hide the projection cards if they don't apply.
