Every projection chart, on the Dashboard and on each account, shows a stat at the top right. Tap it to cycle to the next one. Your choice is remembered.

While you drag across the chart, the stat updates for that month. Otherwise it shows the end of the projection.

## Age

Each person's age at that point, like **65, 62**. Handy for answering "how old will we be when we hit $1M?"

Shown as **Age** for one person and **Ages** for more than one.

## Contributions

The total you'll add through monthly contributions by that point.

The chart adds a gray line for your starting balance plus contributions. The gap between it and the projection is growth: what your money earned on its own.

## Withdrawal

How much income your projected balance could support, like **4% Withdrawal**. It's the balance × your **Safe Withdrawal** rate, shown per month.

Change the rate (2–10%) in **Settings → Charts & Lists → Safe Withdrawal**. Turn on **Withdrawal Per Year** to show a yearly amount. On Net Worth, debts are subtracted first.

## Wealth Multiplier

How many times a single dollar grows by that point, like **5.4x**. It comes from the account's **Expected Return** compounded over the years, so it doesn't depend on the balance or contributions.

Only on individual account projections.

## Interest Paid

For liabilities: the total interest you'll pay before that point.

The chart adds a gray line for interest building up over time. A higher **Monthly Payment** pays a debt off sooner and flattens that line.

Shown on the Liabilities projection and on individual loans, mortgages, and credit cards.

:::note
With **Show in Today's Money** on, Contributions, Withdrawal, Wealth Multiplier, and Interest Paid are adjusted for inflation too.
:::
