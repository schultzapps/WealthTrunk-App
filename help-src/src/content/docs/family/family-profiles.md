WealthTrunk is built for a whole household. Create a profile for each person, then assign accounts, records, and goals to their owner.

## Filter by person

Use the person filter menu to see the whole household, or just one person or a few. Your net worth, charts, and lists update to match.

## Make it personal

- **Pop of Color:** give each family member an accent color. The app takes on that color when you switch between them.
- **Family name:** add your family name to personalize the app.
- **Group by Owner:** group Records and Goals by owner, so everything that belongs to one person appears together.

:::note[Sharing data with a partner]
Family Sharing shares a Pro **subscription**. It doesn't share your **data**. To put your data on a partner's device, use [Backup & Restore](/help/data/backup-restore/) to copy it over. A family syncing feature is on the roadmap.
:::
