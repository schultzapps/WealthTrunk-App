Records keep the important details around your money in one place: who to call, what you're covered for, and what your family should know. Your accounts show what you have. Records explain how it all works.

- **Insurance:** life, home, auto, disability, or umbrella policies, with the company and coverage amount. In an emergency, your family knows what coverage exists and who it's with.
- **Contacts:** your financial advisor, CPA, attorney, or insurance agent, with phone, email, and website. Tap to call, email, or copy.
- **Notes:** anything else worth writing down, like where the will is kept, how bills get paid, or beneficiary decisions.

## Adding a record

1. **Records** tab → **+** (Mac: **⇧ ⌘ R**).
2. Choose **Insurance**, **Contact**, or **Note**.
3. Fill in the details and owner, then **Save**.

Records use the same owners as accounts, so [profile selection](/help/getting-started/finding-your-way/#profile-selection) filters them too. Use **•••** → **Group By** to sort them by type or owner.

## Attachments

Add up to five photos, PDFs, or scans to any record, like a policy declaration page or a signed document.

:::caution
[Backups](/help/data/backup-restore/) don't include attachment files.
:::

## Reminders

Add a **Reminder** to any record, once or repeating, like a yearly policy review or a quarterly check-in with your advisor. Requires notifications: **Settings → Notifications → Allow Reminders**.

## Records in your PDF statement

A [PDF statement](/help/dashboard/pdf-statements/) can include your records as tables after your accounts:

- **Insurance:** institution, owner, policy, and coverage
- **Contacts:** company, owner, name, phone, and email
- **Notes:** title, owner, and the full note

That turns your statement into a single summary of who to call and what's in place, not just what you're worth. It's an easy way to hand a spouse, adult child, or executor everything they'd need.
