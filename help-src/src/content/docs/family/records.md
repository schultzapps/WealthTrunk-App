Records hold **Insurance**, **Contacts**, and **Notes**.

## Adding a record

1. **Records** tab → **+** (Mac: **⇧ ⌘ R**).
2. Choose **Insurance**, **Contact**, or **Note**.
3. Fill in the details and owner, then **Save**.

## Attachments

Add up to five photos, PDFs, or scans to any record.

:::caution
[Backups](/help/data/backup-restore/) don't include attachment files.
:::

## Reminders

Add a **Reminder** to any record, once or repeating. Requires notifications: **Settings → Notifications → Allow Reminders**.

## PDF statements

Choose whether to include records when creating a [PDF statement](/help/data/export/).
