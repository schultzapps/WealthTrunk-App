Your data is yours. You can export it whenever you like, and you're never locked in.

## PDF Net Worth Statement

Create a polished statement to share with an advisor, a lender, or your partner. It includes:

- The charts you have on your Dashboard
- A full or muted color scheme, and a 4- or 5-chart layout
- Any record types you choose to include
- Custom footer text (Pro)

On Mac, the menu bar has keyboard shortcuts for creating a statement.

## CSV export

From **Import/Export Data**, export your accounts, balance history, holdings, and goals to CSV. Open the files in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

An accounts export also gives you the account IDs you need to [import balance history](/help/accounts/import-csv/).
