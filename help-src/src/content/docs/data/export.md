Your data is yours. You can export it whenever you like, and you're never locked in.

## PDF Net Worth Statement

Create a polished statement to share with an advisor, a lender, or your partner.

1. On the **Dashboard**, tap the **Share** button at the top. On Mac, press **⌘ P**.
2. Choose what to include under **Content**: **Financial Summary**, **Accounts**, **Contacts**, **Insurance**, and **Notes**.
3. Adjust the **Formatting**:
   - **Chart Layout** and **Chart Colors** (full or muted)
   - Whether to show **Account Values** and **Account Logos**
   - The **Header Date**
   - **Footer Text**
4. Tap **Create PDF Statement**, then save or share it.

The statement includes the charts you have on your Dashboard, so [customize your Dashboard](/help/dashboard/customize-dashboard/) first to change which charts appear.

:::tip
If the **Share** button shows an **eye** instead, your amounts are hidden. Tap it to show them first.
:::

## CSV export

Go to **Settings → Import/Export Data → Export Data (CSV)**, choose what to export (accounts, balance history, holdings, or goals), and tap **Export**. Open the file in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.

An accounts export also gives you the account IDs you need to [import balance history](/help/accounts/import-csv/).
