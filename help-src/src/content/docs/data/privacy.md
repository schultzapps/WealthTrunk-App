## Where your data lives

WealthTrunk stores your data in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account. It's encrypted, and only you can access it. The developer can't see, read, or access your financial information.

There's no sign-up, and WealthTrunk doesn't collect your name or email address. It never connects to your bank, so there are no bank logins to store.

## Lock the app with Face ID

iOS can do this for any app: touch and hold the WealthTrunk icon on your Home Screen and choose **Require Face ID**.

## Hide amounts on screen

Choose **Hide Amounts** from the **•••** menu on the Accounts, Goals, or Records tab. This is handy when using the app in public. See [Finding your way around](/help/getting-started/finding-your-way/#hide-your-balances).

## Delete your data

Go to **Settings → Import/Export Data → Delete Data**, choose what to delete, and confirm. This removes the data from your device **and** from iCloud, and it can't be undone. Consider [creating a backup](/help/data/backup-restore/) first.
