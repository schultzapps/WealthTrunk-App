## Your data

Stored encrypted in your own iCloud account. There's no sign-up or bank login.

## Face ID lock

Touch and hold the app icon → **Require Face ID**.

## Hide amounts

**•••** menu on Accounts, Goals, or Records → **Hide Amounts**.

## Delete your data

**Settings → Import/Export Data → Delete Data**. This deletes from your devices and iCloud and can't be undone.
