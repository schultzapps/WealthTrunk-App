When you're signed in to iCloud, WealthTrunk syncs your data automatically across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Changes on one device show up on the others within moments. Your core app settings sync too, and they come back if you reinstall the app.

Your data is stored in **your own** iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, encrypted, and only you can access it. WealthTrunk can't see, read, or access your financial information.

## If something isn't syncing

1. Make sure each device is signed in to the **same Apple Account**.
2. Check that iCloud is turned on for WealthTrunk in your device's iCloud settings.
3. Make sure each device has an internet connection, then open WealthTrunk and give it a moment to catch up.

Still stuck? [Contact support](/help/contact/).
