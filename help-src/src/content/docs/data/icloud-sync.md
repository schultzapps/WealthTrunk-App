WealthTrunk syncs automatically to every iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same iCloud account. Changes usually appear on your other devices within a few seconds. Your app settings sync too.

## Turn on iCloud

Open the iPhone **Settings** app, tap your name, then **iCloud**, and make sure **WealthTrunk** is turned on.

## New device or reinstalling

On the first welcome screen, tap **Returning User**. Your data loads from iCloud, which can take 30–60 seconds even on a fast connection.

## Sync status

**Settings → iCloud Sync** shows your iCloud login, storage, item counts, and anything **Pending sync**.

## Not syncing?

1. Use the same Apple Account on every device, with iCloud on for WealthTrunk.
2. Check you have iCloud storage available.
3. Open the app online and wait for **Pending sync** to reach zero.
