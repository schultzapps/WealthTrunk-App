Recording balances is the core habit in WealthTrunk. Each time you update an account, the app saves that balance with its date and adds it to your history.

## How often should I update?

How often is up to you. Some people update weekly or monthly; others quarterly or once a year. Being consistent matters more than how often, so choose a schedule you'll actually keep.

## Update without opening the app

You can record a balance with **Siri**, **Spotlight**, or **Shortcuts**. Say *"Update my balance in WealthTrunk"*, or build a Shortcut to run right after you check your banking app.

## Add past balances

To give your charts years of history from day one, import past balances with the [balance history CSV template](/help/accounts/import-csv/).
