Recording balances is the core habit in WealthTrunk. Each new balance is saved with its date and becomes part of that account's history.

## Update every account at once

1. On the **Accounts** tab, tap **Update All** (on Mac, press **⌘ B**).
2. Work through the list and enter each account's new balance. The progress bar shows how many you've done.
3. If an account hasn't changed, tap its previous balance in the bar above the keyboard instead of retyping it.
4. Tap **Done**.

:::tip
Charts are most accurate when every account is updated on the same date, so a monthly "update everything" session works well.
:::

## Update a single account

Open the account and tap the **pencil** button next to **Account Balance** to add a new entry.

## Update without opening the app

- **Siri:** say *"Update my balance in WealthTrunk"*. Siri asks which account and what the new balance is. You can also name the account: *"Update Chase Checking balance in WealthTrunk"*.
- **Spotlight:** swipe down on your Home Screen, search **Update Balance**, and tap the result. On newer devices, individual accounts may appear for even faster entry.
- **Shortcuts:** add WealthTrunk's **Update Account Balance** action to a shortcut, for example one you run right after checking your banking app.

## Update with a tap on the back of your iPhone

With **Back Tap**, you can double- or triple-tap the back of your iPhone to record a balance from any screen, including your banking app.

**1. Create the shortcut**

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **+** to create a new shortcut.
2. Search for **WealthTrunk** and choose **Update Account Balance**.
3. Leave **Account** and **Amount** empty, and WealthTrunk will ask for both each time it runs. Or pick an **Account** if you always update the same one.
4. Rename the shortcut, for example to "Update Balance", and tap **Done**.

**2. Assign it to Back Tap**

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app and go to **Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap**.
2. Choose **Double Tap** or **Triple Tap**.
3. Scroll down to **Shortcuts** and select the shortcut you just made.

Now double- or triple-tap the back of your iPhone, choose the account, and enter the new balance. WealthTrunk confirms the update without leaving the app you're in.

:::tip
The same shortcut works from the **Action button** on iPhone models that have one: go to **Settings → Action Button**, choose **Shortcut**, and select it.
:::

## Get a reminder

Turn on **Net Worth Reminders** in **Settings → Notifications** and choose how often, which day, and what time.

## How often should I update?

How often is up to you. Some people update weekly or monthly; others quarterly or once a year. Being consistent matters more than how often.

## Keep your balances fresh

Your net worth is only as accurate as your oldest balance. If one account hasn't been updated in months, your totals, charts, and projections all treat that old value as current. WealthTrunk has three ways to spot accounts that are falling behind:

1. **Highlight Stale Accounts.** In **Settings → Charts & Lists**, choose how old is too old: 1 week, 1 month, 6 months (the default), or 1 year. In the **Accounts** list, any account older than that has its "last updated" date shown in your accent color.
2. **Sort by Last Updated.** On the **Accounts** tab, open the **•••** menu and choose **Sort By → Last Updated** to bring the accounts that need attention together.
3. **Staleness Indicator.** Turn this on in **Settings → Charts & Lists** and the Balance History chart shows a dashed line once your latest update is more than 7 days old.

:::tip
Match **Highlight Stale Accounts** to your routine. If you update monthly, set it to 1 month, and anything highlighted is an account you missed.
:::
