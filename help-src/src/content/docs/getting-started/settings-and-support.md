## Open Settings

- **iPhone:** tap your **profile picture** (top right), then **Settings**.
- **iPad and Mac:** **Settings** is in the sidebar. On Mac, **⌘ ,** also works.

Settings applies to the whole app, whichever family member you're viewing. Your app settings sync across your devices with [iCloud](/help/data/icloud-sync/).

## What you'll find in Settings

Tap a row to open it. On iPad and Mac, the page opens beside the list. Rows are grouped by purpose, and the list changes a little with your plan and app version.

- **Personalize:** [family profiles](/help/family/family-profiles/), [custom account and tax types](/help/accounts/account-types/#custom-account-types), [currency](/help/accounts/currency/), [chart and list options](/help/dashboard/customize-dashboard/#chart--list-settings), and appearance.
- **Manage:** [reminders](/help/accounts/recording-balances/), [iCloud sync](/help/data/icloud-sync/), [backup, export, and import](/help/data/backup-restore/), and your purchases.
- **Support:** what's new, Help & FAQ, contacting the developer, and the privacy policy and terms.

## Manage your subscription

You can reach your plan from a few places:

- **The WealthTrunk Pro card.** On the free plan or a trial, it's in Settings, and on iPad and Mac also in the sidebar. Tap it to see plans and upgrade. During a trial it shows your plan status instead.
- **Purchases in Settings.** Once you have Pro, this row shows your plan, when it renews, and your options.
- **When you tap a Pro feature,** or reach the free account limit, the plans appear.

### On the Purchases page

- **View Plans** to switch plans.
- **Manage Subscription** opens your subscriptions in the App Store, where you can change or cancel.
- **Customer ID** is there if support asks for it. Tap **Show**, then **Copy**.

A one-time purchase has no renewal, so it shows fewer options.

### Restore Purchases

On a new device, or after reinstalling, tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings. Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with. The row appears on the free plan, and it's replaced by **Purchases** once you have Pro.

### Cancel

Cancel in Apple's settings, not in WealthTrunk. Use **Manage Subscription** on the Purchases page, or open the iPhone **Settings** app → your name → **Subscriptions** → **WealthTrunk**. Pro lasts until the billing period ends, and nothing is deleted. Billing questions go to Apple.

To see what Pro includes and how Family Sharing works, see [WealthTrunk Pro](/help/subscription/wealthtrunk-pro/).

## Read the FAQ in the app

Open **Help & FAQ** in Settings. On Mac, it's also in the **Help** menu, and **⌘ ?** opens it.

- Use **Search** to find an answer.
- **Help Center** opens this site inside the app.
- The **lightbulb** button shows all tips again on next launch.

## Contact the developer

Tap **Contact Us** in Settings to start an email to support. The app adds some device and app details below your message to help with troubleshooting. You can delete them before sending. Don't include account numbers or balances.

If no email app is set up, the app shows the support address so you can copy it and write from any mail app. On Mac, **Help → Get Help** also starts an email to support.

For an idea rather than a problem, use **Suggest Feature** if it's in your Settings.
