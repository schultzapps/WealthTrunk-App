## Open Settings

- **iPhone:** tap your **profile picture** (top right), then **Settings**.
- **iPad and Mac:** **Settings** is in the sidebar. On Mac, **⌘ ,** also works.

Settings applies to the whole app, whichever family member you're viewing. Your app settings sync across your devices with [iCloud](/help/data/icloud-sync/).

## What's in Settings

Settings is a list in three groups. Tap a row to open it. On iPad and Mac, the page opens beside the list.

### Personalize

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Family Members** | Add and edit [profiles](/help/family/family-profiles/). Once you set a family name, this row shows it, like **Smith Family**. |
| **Custom Accounts** | Create your own [account types](/help/accounts/account-types/#custom-account-types) and [tax types](/help/accounts/tax-types/). |
| **Currency** | Choose the [currency](/help/accounts/currency/) for totals, charts, and goals. |
| **Charts & Lists** | [Chart and list options](/help/dashboard/customize-dashboard/#chart--list-settings), including inflation and safe withdrawal. |
| **Appearance** | Match your device, or always use light or dark. |

### Manage

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | [Net worth reminders](/help/accounts/recording-balances/) and record reminders. |
| **iCloud Sync** | Check [sync status](/help/data/icloud-sync/). |
| **Import/Export Data** | [Back up and restore](/help/data/backup-restore/), [export](/help/data/export/), [import a CSV](/help/accounts/import-csv/), or [delete your data](/help/data/privacy/). |
| **Purchases** | Your Pro plan. Only shown if you have Pro. |
| **Restore Purchases** | Get Pro back on a new device. Only shown on the free plan. |

### Support

| Row | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **What's New** | What changed in the latest version. |
| **Help & FAQ** | Search answers in the app, or open this Help Center. |
| **Suggest Feature** | Send an idea. Shown when the developer has it turned on. |
| **Tell A Friend** | Share a link to WealthTrunk. |
| **Contact Us** | Email the developer. |
| **Rate This App** | Open the App Store review page. |
| **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service** | Read them in the app. |

## Manage your subscription

Where to look depends on your plan.

| Your plan | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | Tap the **WealthTrunk Pro** card at the top of Settings to see the plans and upgrade. On iPad and Mac the same card sits at the bottom of the sidebar. It shows your account count, like **Free Plan 8/10**. |
| **Free trial** | The same card shows how many trial days are left. Tap it to open **Purchases**. |
| **Pro** | **Settings → Manage → Purchases**. |

You'll also see the plans when you tap a Pro feature, or when you reach the free account limit.

### On the Purchases page

- **Plan**, and when it **Renews** or **Expires**.
- **View Plans:** switch to a different plan.
- **Manage Subscription:** opens your subscriptions in the App Store, where you can change or cancel.
- **Customer ID:** tap **Show**, then **Copy**. Support may ask for it.

A one-time purchase has no renewal date, so it shows only the plan and your Customer ID.

### Restore Purchases

On a new device, or after reinstalling: **Settings → Manage → Restore Purchases**. You'll see **Pro access restored successfully!**, or a message that no purchase was found. Sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Once you have Pro, the row is replaced by **Purchases**.

### Cancel

Cancel in Apple's settings, not in WealthTrunk. Either tap **Manage Subscription** on the Purchases page, or open the iPhone **Settings** app → your name → **Subscriptions** → **WealthTrunk**. Pro lasts until the billing period ends, and nothing is deleted. Billing questions go to Apple.

To see what Pro includes, how Family Sharing works, and what happens after you cancel, see [WealthTrunk Pro](/help/subscription/wealthtrunk-pro/).

## Read the FAQ in the app

**Settings → Support → Help & FAQ.** On Mac, use **Help → Help & FAQ** or **⌘ ?**.

- Type in the **Search** field to find an answer. Matching answers open automatically.
- Answers are grouped as **General**, **Tracking Accounts**, **Goals**, **FIRE**, **Calculations**, **Purchases & Subscriptions**, and **Privacy**.
- **Help Center** at the top opens this site inside the app.
- The **lightbulb** button, top right, shows all tips again on next launch.
- Your **Customer ID** is at the bottom.

## Contact the developer

**Settings → Support → Contact Us.** It opens a new email addressed to support, with the subject **WealthTrunk Support Request**. Below your message, the app adds a few details that help with troubleshooting: your device model, OS version, language, currency, time zone, app version, Customer ID, and plan. Delete any of it before sending if you'd like. Don't include account numbers or balances.

On Mac, **Help → Get Help** also opens an email to support, but without those details.

If no email app is set up, the app shows **No Email Configured** with a **Copy Email Address** button, so you can write from any mail app.

Before you write, it's worth a quick search in **Help & FAQ** or this Help Center. For an idea rather than a problem, use **Suggest Feature** if it's in your Support list.
