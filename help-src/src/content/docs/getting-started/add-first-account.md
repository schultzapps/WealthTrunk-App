Every account you add is either an **asset** (something you own) or a **liability** (something you owe). Your net worth is your assets minus your liabilities.

## Add an account

1. Open the **Accounts** tab and tap **+**.
2. Search for your bank or institution by name. WealthTrunk attaches the institution's name and logo for you.
3. Pick an account type, such as checking, brokerage, or mortgage.
4. Enter the current balance and save.

:::tip
For property like a home, vehicle, or collectible, use the property's own name instead of a bank name, for example "Lake House" or "2019 Outback".
:::

## Adding a lot of accounts?

You don't have to add them one at a time. Fill in a spreadsheet and [import it from CSV](/help/accounts/import-csv/) instead.

## Customize it

- Use an emoji as an account's logo.
- If no built-in account type or tax type fits, create your own right from the picker. Custom types are free for everyone.
- Assign the account to a [family member](/help/family/family-profiles/) so you can see each person's net worth separately.
