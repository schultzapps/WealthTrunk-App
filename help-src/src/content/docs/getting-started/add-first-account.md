Every account you add is either an **asset** (something you own) or a **liability** (something you owe). Your net worth is your assets minus your liabilities.

## Add an account

1. Open the **Accounts** tab and tap **+** (**Add an Account**). On Mac, press **⌘ N**.
2. Under **Find or add an institution**, search for your bank. WealthTrunk fills in the institution's name and logo for you.
3. Choose an **Account Type**, such as Checking, Brokerage, or Mortgage. The account's **Tax Status** is filled in for you, and you can change it.
4. Choose the account's owner. Pick both people for a joint account.
5. Enter the current balance and the **Balance Date**, then tap **Save**.

:::tip
For property like a home, vehicle, or collectible, there's no bank to search for. Give it its own name instead, for example "Our Home" or "2019 Outback".
:::

## Optional details

These are worth filling in, because they drive your projections:

- **Assets:** **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**
- **Liabilities:** **Loan Rate (APR)** and **Monthly Payment**
- **Include In Projections:** turn this off for accounts you don't want projected forward
- **Nickname**, **Currency**, **Notes**, and a custom logo (tap **Change Logo**, or use an emoji)

## Adding a lot of accounts?

You don't have to add them one at a time. Fill in a spreadsheet and [import it from CSV](/help/accounts/import-csv/) instead.

## How many accounts can I add?

The free plan includes up to **10 accounts**. The Accounts tab shows your count as **Free Plan 3/10**, for example. [WealthTrunk Pro](/help/subscription/free-vs-pro/) removes the limit.
