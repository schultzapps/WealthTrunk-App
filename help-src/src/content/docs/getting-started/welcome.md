WealthTrunk is a manual net worth tracker for your household. You add accounts and update balances; the app shows your net worth, its history, and where it's headed.

## Why manual?

- No bank logins or third parties
- No broken connections or gaps in your history
- Updating by hand keeps you aware of your money

## Your data

Stored in your own iCloud account and synced across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No sign-up, and we can't see it.
