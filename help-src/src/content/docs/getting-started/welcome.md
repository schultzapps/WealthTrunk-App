WealthTrunk is a manual net worth tracker for your whole household. You add your accounts and update their balances yourself. The app then shows your net worth, how it has changed, and where it's headed.

## How it works

1. **Add your accounts.** Include everything you own and owe: checking, savings, investments, retirement accounts, real estate, vehicles, loans, and credit cards.
2. **Record balances.** Every so often, enter each account's current balance. Each entry becomes part of your history.
3. **Watch the trend.** The Dashboard turns that history into charts, insights, and projections.

## Why manual?

WealthTrunk never connects to your bank, so:

- There are no bank logins to store and no third parties handling your data.
- Your history doesn't break or go missing when a bank connection fails.
- A few minutes spent updating balances keeps you aware of your money, and that awareness tends to lead to better decisions.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It syncs across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. WealthTrunk has no sign-up and no account to create, and we can't see your financial information.
