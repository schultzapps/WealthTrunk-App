WealthTrunk has four main tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your net worth, charts, projections, and insights |
| **Accounts** | Every asset and liability, where you record balances |
| **Goals** | Net worth, savings, and debt goals |
| **Records** | Insurance policies, contacts, and notes |

On iPad and Mac, the tabs appear in a sidebar.

## Opening Settings

- **iPhone:** tap your **profile picture** at the top right of any tab. If you have more than one profile, this opens the profile filter; tap **Settings** at the bottom of it.
- **iPad and Mac:** **Settings** is in the sidebar. On Mac you can also press **⌘ ,**.

## Hide your balances

When using the app in public, open the **•••** menu on the Accounts, Goals, or Records tab and choose **Hide Amounts**. To show them again, tap the **eye** button on the Dashboard, or choose **Show Amounts** from the same menu.

## Mac keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **⌘ B** | Update All Accounts |
| **⌘ N** | Add an Account |
| **⇧ ⌘ N** | Add Profile |
| **⇧ ⌘ R** | Add a Record |
| **⌘ P** | Create PDF Statement |
| **⇧ ⌘ I** | Import/Export Data |
| **⌘ ?** | Help & FAQ |

## Bring back the in-app tips

Go to **Settings → Help & FAQ**, then tap the **lightbulb** button at the top. Tips show again the next time the app launches.
