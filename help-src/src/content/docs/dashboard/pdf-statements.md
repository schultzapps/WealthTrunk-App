A PDF statement is a clean, printable snapshot of your finances on one date: your net worth, charts, every account, and your records.

## Why create one

- **Keep your family in the loop.** Many households have one person who handles the money. A statement shows your spouse or partner the full picture without them needing the app.
- **Be ready for an emergency.** With [records](/help/family/records/) included, it lists your accounts, insurance, and key contacts in one place. Keep a copy with your will or estate documents.
- **Meet with an advisor.** Bring a statement to a financial advisor, CPA, or attorney instead of gathering numbers by hand.
- **Track progress.** Save one each year or quarter to look back on.

## Creating a statement

1. **Dashboard** → **Share Statement** (top right). On Mac, **File → Create PDF Statement** (**⌘ P**).
2. Choose **Content** and **Formatting**.
3. Tap **Create PDF Statement**, then share, save to Files, or print.

The statement covers the [people you've selected](/help/getting-started/finding-your-way/#profile-selection), like "for Chris and Taylor". Select one person to make a statement just for them.

## Content

Turn each section on or off:

| Setting | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **Financial Summary** | Your net worth totals and charts |
| **Accounts** | Every account and its balance |
| **Contacts** | Your contact records |
| **Insurance** | Your insurance records |
| **Notes** | Your note records |

## Formatting

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Chart Layout** | Show **4** or **5** charts. They're the first charts on your Dashboard, in the same order. |
| **Chart Colors** | **Full** color or **Single** color |
| **Account Values** | Turn off to share the list of accounts and records without any amounts. |
| **Account Logos** | Show institution logos next to accounts and records. |
| **Header Date** | The date printed at the top. Only changes the label, not the balances. |
| **Footer Text** | Your own line at the bottom of every page, up to 50 characters. |

Tap **Reset** to go back to the default settings.
