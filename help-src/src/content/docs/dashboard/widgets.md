---
title: Widgets
description: See your net worth, accounts, and goals on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---
Add from your Home Screen: touch and hold → **Edit → Add Widget** → **WealthTrunk**.

| Widget | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Net Worth** | Net worth, optionally with a chart. Also on the Lock Screen. |
| **Accounts** | Chosen account balances |
| **Goal Card** | One goal |
| **Goal List** | Up to 3 goals |

Touch and hold a widget → **Edit Widget** to choose accounts, goals, or people.
