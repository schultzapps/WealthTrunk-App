title: Widgets
description: See your net worth, accounts, and goals on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.
sidebar:

---

Add a WealthTrunk widget the same way as any other: touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget**, and search for **WealthTrunk**.

| Widget | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Net Worth** | Your net worth, with or without a trend chart |
| **Accounts** | Balances for accounts you choose |
| **Goal Card** | Progress toward one goal |
| **Goal List** | Progress on up to 3 goals |

A smaller **Net Worth** widget is also available for the Lock Screen.

To change which accounts, goals, or family members a widget shows, touch and hold it and choose **Edit Widget**. Tapping a goal in a widget opens it in the app.
