The Dashboard brings your net worth, trends, and insights together, and you decide what goes on it.

## Available cards

| Card | Shows | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Net Worth Cards** | Your totals at a glance | On |
| **Balance History** | Net worth over time | On |
| **Balance Projection** | Where your net worth is headed | On |
| **Net Worth Breakdown** | Assets vs. liabilities by group (bar or donut) | On |
| **Tax Categories** | Wealth by tax treatment (bar or donut) | On |
| **Goals** | Progress on your goals | Off |
| **Monthly Net Worth** | Assets and liabilities side by side each month | Off |
| **Portfolio Allocation** (Pro) | Your [holdings](/help/accounts/holdings/) as a tree map | Off |
| **FIRE** (Pro) | Progress toward [financial independence](/help/dashboard/projections/#financial-independence-fire) | Off |

## Customize

Scroll to the bottom of the Dashboard and tap **Customize**. From there you can:

- Show or hide cards (**Visible** and **Hidden**)
- Drag cards into a new order
- Set the **Chart Size**: Small, Medium, Large, or XL
- Choose one or two **Columns**
- **Reset Layout** to start over

## Quick changes from a card

**Long press any card** to:

- **Hide** it
- Switch its **Chart Type**, for example between bar and donut
- Change its **View Style**, for example between card and list for Goals

Tap a chart to explore its details and insights.

## More chart settings

**Settings → Charts & Lists** has more options:

- **Line Smoothing** for Balance History
- **Months Displayed** and **Estimate Month-End Value** for Monthly Net Worth. You can also pinch the chart to zoom.
- **Inflation Settings** and **Safe Withdrawal** for projections
- **Show One Currency**, which converts foreign-currency accounts to your app currency

## Share it

Tap the **Share** button at the top of the Dashboard to create a [PDF Net Worth Statement](/help/data/export/) that includes the charts on your Dashboard.
