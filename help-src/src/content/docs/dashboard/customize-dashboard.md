## Cards

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Net Worth Cards** | Your totals |
| **Balance History** | Net worth over time |
| **Balance Projection** | Projected net worth |
| **Net Worth Breakdown** | Assets and liabilities by [account group](/help/accounts/account-types/) |
| **Tax Categories** | Wealth by [tax type](/help/accounts/tax-types/) |
| **Goals** | [Goal](/help/goals/) progress |
| **Monthly Net Worth** | Assets vs. liabilities each month |
| **Portfolio Allocation** | [Holdings](/help/holdings/) as a tree map |
| **FIRE** | Progress toward [financial independence](/help/dashboard/projections/#fire) |

## Customize

Tap **Customize** at the bottom of the Dashboard to show, hide, and reorder cards, set **Chart Size** and **Columns**, or **Reset Layout**.

Long press a card to **Hide** it or change its **Chart Type** or **View Style**.

## Chart & list settings

In **Settings → Charts & Lists**:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Line Smoothing** | Curved lines between updates. Off shows straight lines. |
| **Staleness Indicator** | Dashed line on Balance History when your latest update is over 7 days old. |
| **Show in Today's Money** / **Inflation Rate** | Shows projections [adjusted for inflation](/help/dashboard/projections/#todays-money). |
| **Safe Withdrawal** | Rate (default 4%) used to estimate income from your projected balance. |
| **Withdrawal Per Year** | Shows that income yearly instead of monthly. |
| **Months Displayed** | Months on the Monthly Net Worth chart (1–12). Pinch to zoom. |
| **Estimate Month-End Value** | Each column shows your net worth on the last day of the month, but manual updates rarely land on that day. If you updated on the 20th and again on the 5th of the next month, this estimates the 31st from the trend between the two. Off uses your last update before month-end, so a month can look flat or lumpy depending on when you updated. |
| **Highlight Stale Accounts** | Colors accounts not updated within a set period. See [Keep balances fresh](/help/getting-started/record-balances/#keep-balances-fresh). |
| **Show One Currency** | Shows foreign accounts in your app currency. |
