The Dashboard brings your net worth, trends, and insights together on one screen, and you decide what goes on it.

## Quick gestures

- **Long press a card** to hide it or switch its chart type, for example from bar to donut.
- **Tap a chart** to see more detail and insights.
- **Long press a group header** to collapse or expand that group.

## Customize window

Use **Customize** to:

- Add or remove cards
- Reorder everything
- Resize charts: Small, Medium, Large, or XL

On wider screens, such as iPad, Mac, or iPhone in landscape, the Dashboard switches to two columns.

## More chart settings

**Settings → Charts** has further options, such as smoothing balance history charts and **Estimate Month-End Value** for the Monthly Net Worth chart.

## Hide your numbers

Turn on **Privacy mode** to hide your balances, for example when using the app in public.
