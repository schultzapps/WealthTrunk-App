The Dashboard brings your net worth, trends, and insights together, and you decide what goes on it.

## Available cards

These cards are on the Dashboard to start with:

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Net Worth Cards** | Your totals at a glance |
| **Balance History** | Net worth over time |
| **Balance Projection** | Where your net worth is headed |
| **Net Worth Breakdown** | Assets vs. liabilities by group (bar or donut) |
| **Tax Categories** | Wealth by tax treatment (bar or donut) |

### Pro cards

These optional cards can be added with a [Pro subscription](/help/subscription/free-vs-pro/):

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals** | Progress on your [goals](/help/goals/) |
| **Monthly Net Worth** | Assets and liabilities side by side each month |
| **Portfolio Allocation** | Your [holdings](/help/accounts/holdings/) as a tree map |
| **FIRE** | Progress toward [financial independence](/help/dashboard/projections/#financial-independence-fire) |

## Customize

Scroll to the bottom of the Dashboard and tap **Customize**. From there you can:

- Show or hide cards (**Visible** and **Hidden**)
- Drag cards into a new order
- Set the **Chart Size**: Small, Medium, Large, or XL
- Choose one or two **Columns**
- **Reset Layout** to start over

## Quick changes from a card

**Long press any card** to:

- **Hide** it
- Switch its **Chart Type**, for example between bar and donut
- Change its **View Style**, for example between card and list for Goals

Tap a chart to explore its details and insights.

## Chart & list settings

Go to **Settings → Charts & Lists** to fine-tune how charts and lists behave.

### Balance History

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Line Smoothing** | Draws the net worth line as a smooth curve between your balance updates. Turn it off to connect them with straight lines, which shows exactly when each change happened. On by default. |
| **Staleness Indicator** | If your latest balances are more than 7 days old, the chart extends a **dashed flat line** from your last update to today. This is a reminder that the most recent stretch of the chart is carried forward, not newly recorded. Off by default. |

### Growth Projections

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Show in Today's Money** and **Inflation Rate** | Shows projections adjusted for inflation, in what the money would buy today. See [Projections & inflation](/help/dashboard/projections/#show-in-todays-money). |
| **Safe Withdrawal** | The withdrawal rate (2% to 10%, default 4%) behind the projection chart's income insight. This estimates how much income your future balance could provide each year. |
| **Withdrawal Per Year** | Shows that estimated income per year instead of per month. |

### Monthly Net Worth

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Months Displayed** | How many months the chart shows, from 1 to 12 (default 6). You can also pinch the chart to zoom. |
| **Estimate Month-End Value** | If you didn't record a balance on the last day of a month, estimates that month's closing value from the updates on either side, so each column reflects the month's end. On by default. |

### Accounts List

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Highlight Stale Accounts** | Colors an account's "last updated" date in the Accounts list when it hasn't been updated in a chosen period: 1 week, 1 month, 6 months (default), 1 year, or Never. See [Keep your balances fresh](/help/getting-started/record-balances/#keep-your-balances-fresh). |
| **Show One Currency** | Shows foreign-currency accounts converted to your app currency in the Accounts list, instead of in their own currency. |
