WealthTrunk projects each account forward from its current balance using the rates and contributions you set on it. Projections run to the latest **Retirement Age** among your primary profiles.

## What drives a projection

- **Assets:** **Expected Return** and **Monthly Contribution**
- **Liabilities:** **Loan Rate (APR)** and **Monthly Payment**. Once a debt reaches zero, it stays at zero.
- **Include In Projections:** turn this off for any account you want left out. Future Income accounts are always left out of growth projections.

## Projection Simulator

Every account has a **Projection Simulator**. Use its sliders to try out "what if" scenarios, such as a higher contribution or a faster debt paydown, without changing your real data.

## Contributions that grow each year

Any account with a monthly contribution has a slider below the contribution amount that sets how the contribution changes each year:

- With inflation **off**, it reads **Annual Change**. Set 2% and the contribution grows 2% a year.
- With inflation **on**, it reads **± vs. Inflation**. At 0% the contribution keeps pace with inflation; +1% grows it 1% faster than inflation.

Most of the time, leave this slider at 0%.

## Show in Today's Money

By default, projections show **nominal** values: the actual number you'd see in the account on that future date. To see what that money would actually buy:

1. Go to **Settings → Charts & Lists → Inflation Settings**.
2. Set your **Inflation Rate** and turn on **Show in Today's Money**.

For example, a projected $1,000,000 in 30 years with 2.5% inflation reads as about **$477,000** in today's money.

:::caution
Keep each account's **Expected Return** as a normal (nominal) rate, like 7% or 10%. Don't subtract inflation from it yourself, or inflation gets counted twice.
:::

## Partners and dependents

- **Joint accounts** keep receiving contributions until the last primary profile retires.
- **Individual accounts** stop receiving contributions at their owner's retirement age, but keep growing until the last primary profile retires.
- **Dependents** (such as children) have their own **Savings Age** and don't affect the retirement timeline.

## Financial independence (FIRE)

The **FIRE** card (Pro) estimates when your investments could cover your spending.

- **FIRE number** = (monthly spending × 12) ÷ withdrawal rate. For example, $5,000 a month at a 4% withdrawal rate is $1,500,000.
- **Coast FIRE** is the amount you'd need invested today for growth alone to reach your FIRE number by retirement. Tap the card's title to switch between FIRE and Coast FIRE.
- Tap the card's **gear** button to set each primary profile's **Monthly Spending** and your withdrawal rate. Spending is added together for the household. The card only works for primary profiles, not dependents.

The FIRE card is a simplified estimate. It uses one blended return across all your accounts and a single retirement date, and it doesn't account for taxes, so its numbers won't exactly match your other projections.

## Already retired?

Projections only run while a profile's retirement (or savings) age is still ahead of them. If that doesn't apply to you, you can hide the projection cards from your Dashboard.
