WealthTrunk projects each account forward from its current balance, its expected return or interest rate, and your monthly contributions.

## Projection Simulator

Every account has a **Projection Simulator**. Use its sliders to try out growth scenarios or a debt paydown plan without changing your real data.

## Growing contributions

Set how your monthly contributions change each year: either a fixed increase every year, or an increase that keeps pace with inflation.

## Nominal vs. Real

Set an inflation rate, then use the **Nominal ↔ Real** toggle. **Real** shows your projected wealth in today's dollars, which is what it would actually buy.

## Financial independence (FIRE)

The FIRE card on the Dashboard shows when your liquid assets are projected to cover your retirement spending. Set your monthly spending and withdrawal rate, drag along the curve to any year, and switch between **FIRE** and **Coast FIRE**.
