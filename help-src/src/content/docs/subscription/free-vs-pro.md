WealthTrunk is free to download, with no account required.

## Free

- Track up to **10 accounts**
- Create profiles for family members
- Net worth charts and projections
- Record historical balances
- Custom account and tax types
- Export PDF statements

## Pro

- **Unlimited** accounts
- Advanced charts and insights, such as **Portfolio Allocation** and **FIRE Progress**
- Custom footer text on PDF statements

## Family Sharing

If you subscribe and have Apple Family Sharing turned on, up to **5 family members** get WealthTrunk Pro at no extra cost.

Family Sharing shares the subscription, not your financial data. See [Family profiles](/help/family/family-profiles/) for how to share data with a partner.
