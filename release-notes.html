Version 1.3.11

July 29, 2026

  • Grouped goal sections
  • Smoother date range picker
  • Mac fixes for backup and CSV import, plus standard improvements

July 17, 2026
  • Inflation support is here!
    • See your future net worth in today's dollars with a new Nominal ↔ Real toggle
    • Set an inflation rate and instantly view what your projected wealth is really worth over time

  • Core app settings now sync across your devices (or after reinstalling), plus refined projection sliders, smoother onboarding, and standard improvements

  • Tap a shared backup file and import it straight into the app
  • Backups now carry your profile photos and custom logos over to a new device
  • Plus standard improvements and bug fixes
