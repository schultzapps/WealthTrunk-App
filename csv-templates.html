CSV Import Templates

Accounts template

Creates accounts in bulk — name, institution, type, balance, contributions and rates. This is the one to start with, and the only one you need if you're setting up from scratch.

Balance history template

Backfills historical balances for accounts that already exist, so your charts show years of history instead of starting from today. Export your accounts from the app first — every row needs an account ID that only an export can give you.

Download balances template

Don't want to fill in a spreadsheet? The net worth calculator builds an accounts file for you from a handful of fields.

How to import

Fill in the template

Open it in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Keep the header row exactly as it is, add one row per account, and save as CSV. The # comment lines at the top are ignored on import — you can leave them.

Get the file onto your device

iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or email it to yourself — anywhere the Files app can reach.

Settings → Import Data (CSV)

Choose Accounts or Balances at the top, tap Import, and select your file.

Review before confirming

Imported accounts wait under Pending Imports so you can assign owners and check the details. Nothing joins your net worth until you say so.
